How to Compress an Image Online

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. You can use this online image compressor for free without installing any software.

Can I see image size in KB and MB?

Yes. The tool displays the original and compressed image sizes in both KB and MB, along with the percentage of size reduction.

Which image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPEG, PNG and WebP images when your browser can decode the selected image.

Will the compressed image always be smaller?

Not always. The final size depends on the original image, format and compression quality. Some PNG or WebP images may become larger when converted to JPEG.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This tool performs compression locally in your browser. Your image is not sent to our server by this page.
