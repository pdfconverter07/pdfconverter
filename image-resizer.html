How to Resize an Image

Resize your images using popular aspect ratios such as 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 16:9 and 9:16, or enter custom dimensions.

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Choose an aspect ratio or enter custom dimensions.
  3. Select pixels or centimeters as the unit.
  4. Click "Resize & Download".
  5. Your resized image will download automatically.
